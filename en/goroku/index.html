RYOZO'S WORDS

Ryozo's Sayings

Words that slipped out on the mat and in everyday conversation.
Collected by Ai Murata

Way of LifeLIFE

If you don't do what you hate, you won't know what you love.

Think about how you spend your life's time.

The word for going all out, "hisshi," is written "certain death."

Live like you're going to die tomorrow.

I think life is nothing but stories you can write down.

Thoughts become reality.

Things only exist beyond what you think and feel.

What you look at and what you think about, that's what matters.

Brainwash yourself.

Live as you are.

Don't live by someone else's idea of happiness.

Things won't make you happy.

Go all out at being alive.

Easy and fun are not the same thing.

Failing is training for the soul.

Moving toward setting your soul free is a good thing.

Live to eat. Eat to live.

Climbing a mountain is a lot like life.

It's hard to make yourself climb alone, and it's even better with someone climbing beside you.

Look your best especially where no one can see.

Competition and Jiu-JitsuFIGHT

Weakness turns into self-pity. Strength turns into confidence.

Think "I can't win unless I do this" and you won't win.

That's making up your own rules, like "I must never do this before a match." There's a limit, but being too rigid is nonsense.

When it comes to jiu-jitsu, and only jiu-jitsu, Ryozo is negative.

Thinking negatively is how you turn it into wins.

In a match, emotions are a waste.

You don't get stronger because you think you're not cut out for it.

In jiu-jitsu, if you're too physically strong, things can work even when you do them wrong.

= You get hurt more easily.

When you jump, you always crouch first, right?

You have to know the minimum strength you need.

You're at your strongest when you're relaxed.

You don't need to tap into pain. Tap into the feeling that something's a little off.

In people, kindness lives inside strength.

Work and BusinessWORK

Having long-term goals matters.

Only coming to train right before a match? "???" People tend to set short-term goals at work too.

A company president should be someone people like, not someone people hate.

The more complicated it gets, the less it works.

Humans are creatures who want to satisfy their desires. Keep that in mind when you do business.

Never work without a sense of tension.

No tension means you're depending on someone.

In business, the best relationships are diagonal, not vertical.

What you do without even meaning to can become a business.

If it doesn't exist, make it.

Not "I'll do it because it looks like it'll work," but "I'll do it until it works."

When starting something, a lot of people do it because it looks like it'll work, but that's wrong. Because you're definitely going to hit a wall.

Whatever it is, decide to be number one.

It's fun, so it makes money.

"I want to keep going until I can do it."

What would happen if you ran your business with that same seriousness? Go all in on jiu-jitsu, then go all in on business with that same seriousness, and see what happens.

If you go all in yourself, you'll become able to save people who are struggling.

People who are good at putting themselves out there succeed.

RelationshipsPEOPLE

Live with no line between you and others.

People with no line feel good to be around.

Only tell someone something at the moment they need it.

You may notice something in your head and still not say it, because it isn't the moment that person needs it.

A true friend is someone who helps you when you're in trouble.

Live so that others owe you, not so that you owe them.

The people around you are what show who you are.

Meaning the people you spend your time with.

Don't change how you act depending on the person.

Don't deal with people based on what you'll gain.

Because in the end, you lose.

Don't pretend you can do things just to get along with people.

Nobody can be liked by everyone, so just be liked by the people you want to be liked by.

You work hard not for yourself, but for others.

Truly funny people don't get laughed at. They make people laugh.

It's no use telling someone who can't see it, "Look, you can see it."

Learning and GrowthGROW

When you think you're slipping, you actually aren't.

What you love is where you can most easily become first-class.

Find a teacher who suits you.

Doing something you hate only creates stress.

Too many people can't hit the gas unless they feel safe.

Doing something you hate means numbing your nerves.

Everyone really hates it, but they try not to feel that they hate it.

What you want from the inside and what you want from the outside are different.

Inside = what you truly love and want to do from the heart. Outside = just things you seem to like. For Ryozo, inside is fashion and outside is poker.

A lot of people really want to change, yet think they don't want to.

Be hungry when you learn from someone.

An insight that doesn't change what you do isn't an insight.

Just correct course right away.

Otherwise you can't tell what's "wrong."

If you decide things by your own standards, you won't grow.

It's no good to just study and feel like you understand.

Happiness and VibesVIBES

To meet people with high vibes, you need vibes of your own, or you won't attract them.

People with high vibes can tell who's low. People with low vibes can't tell who's high.

Low frequencies can be seen. High ones can't.

People who lift you up when you meet them have high vibes.

Even when you think you're "giving," you're "being given."

It's fun, so I don't get tired.

Having fun is the greatest happiness.

Only humans can laugh.

Don't accept hard things as hard.

Even a hard trial has good in it.

Enjoy that trial, and another good trial will come your way.

Don't turn a crisis into an opportunity.

"Ryozo-sensei's answers are always simple."

── by Shuya
