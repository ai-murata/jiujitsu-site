Introduction

Even if you’ve barely touched a computer, Claude becomes your work partner “just by talking to it.” I handle accounting, PR, and event prep for a jiu-jitsu gym all by myself, and starting to use Claude changed how I work in a big way.

This page walks you through it in order: buying a computer, signing up for Claude, connecting your phone, and getting good at using it in daily work.

White belt: Pick a computer

Picking a computer

Claude runs in a browser or an app, so you don’t need a powerful computer. If you’re not sure, a portable laptop with 16 GB of memory or more is plenty.

What to checkTargetWhy

If your phone is an iPhone, go with a Mac. If it’s Android, go with Windows. Moving photos and files around gets a little easier.

Blue belt: Create an account

Creating a Claude account

All you need is an email address or a Google account, and signing up takes about 5 minutes. We recommend trying the free plan first and switching to a paid plan when it starts to feel limiting.

  1. Open claude.ai in your browser
  2. Choose “Continue with Google” or sign up with your email address
  3. Enter your name and what you’d like Claude to call you
  4. Try typing “Hi, what can you do?” in the input box

Plans can change, so check the official pricing page for the latest details.

Purple belt: Connect your phone

See the same chats on computer and phone

Just log in to the same account on your computer and phone, and your chats show up on both automatically. No special setup needed.

  1. Computer: Download the desktop app from claude.ai and install it (you can also just use the browser)
  2. Phone: Search for “Claude” on the App Store or Google Play and install it
  3. Log in on both with the same email address (or Google account)
  4. Check that what you said on your computer shows up in the phone app too

Here’s how I use it: when something occurs to me on the go, I talk it through on my phone. Then when I get to the office, I pick up the same chat on my computer and turn it into a document. The phone app also takes voice input, so it works like a notepad even while I’m walking.

Brown belt: Try it out

Good things to try first

The quickest way in is to hand over one “small job you always do yourself.”

What you wantWhat to say
Draft an email“Write an email apologizing to a client for sending an invoice late. Don’t make it too formal.”
Social media post“Make 3 versions of an Instagram post announcing next week’s jiu-jitsu tournament.”
Summarize a documentAttach a PDF and say “Sum up the important parts of this contract in 5 lines.”
Organize a spreadsheetAttach an Excel file and say “Tally each member’s monthly visits.”
Look something up“Give me a rough idea of how a nonprofit becomes a public interest foundation.”

When the answer is close but not quite there, just add “shorter” or “more casual,” and it’ll fix it.

Black belt: Master it

Tips for mastering it

The keys are these three: “give background,” “keep a separate room for each job,” and “connect your everyday tools.”

  • How to ask: Add a few words on who it’s for, what it’s for, and how long it should be, and the answers get better.
  • Projects: Make a project for each job, like “payroll transfers” or “event announcements,” and put your go-to documents in it. Then you don’t have to explain everything each time.
  • Memory: Turn on memory in the settings, and Claude will remember your work and preferences and use them in future chats.
  • Connectors: Connect Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive, and you can ask directly, like “What’s on tomorrow?” or “Summarize last week’s emails.”
  • Making files: Say “Turn this into slides” or “Put this in Excel,” and it makes a file you can use right away.

You don’t need to write the perfect instructions from the start. Just ask, then keep saying “Fix this part” on what comes back. That’s the fastest way to get better.

Summary

Buy a laptop, sign up at claude.ai, and put the app on your phone. You can do all that in one day. After that, just ask Claude to do one of your jobs each day. A month from now, “I’ll just ask Claude” should come naturally.
