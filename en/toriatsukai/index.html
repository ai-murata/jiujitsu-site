Before You Begin

He's a jiu-jitsu teacher, a company president, and he still competes today. He's definitely an impressive person, but living and working beside him takes a few small tricks.

I'm his wife. I've watched him up close for many years, and I've written down what I know. Here and there, his own side of the story is mixed in too.

02Mornings

MORNING

Mornings are slow. Rushing him won't speed things up.

When he gets up, the first thing he has is coffee. Lately his favorite is adding milk. Until he finishes it, it's best to leave him alone.

At night, he's done the moment he gets sleepy. There's no warning.

03When He's Strongest

UNDER PRESSURE

When he's under pressure. The more cornered he is, the stronger he gets.

Keep him somewhere easy and he'll have power left over. Big stages, key moments, situations with no way out. Those are exactly where you can trust him without holding back.

In his own words

Not "I'll do it because it looks like it'll work," but "I'll do it until it works."

04How to Ask Him Things

HOW TO ASK

He's moved by people. Bring him something as "a task anyone could do" and you won't get much of a reaction.

  1. Bring it to him as "only you can solve this""Ryozo-sensei, I think only you can solve this. What should I do?" Whether or not you say that one line makes a completely different response.
  2. For everyday things, have your own answer firstDon't just toss him the whole thing. Think it through to the point of "Which is best: A, B or C?" and then bring it to him.
  3. When something fell through, bring an alternative with itDon't stop at reporting that it failed. Bring the next move too and things go much faster.

I couldn't get the reservation.

I couldn't get it, so how about this one instead?

We were out of clear tape.

We were out of clear tape, but we do have packing tape. Want me to run to the shop next door?

Sometimes the answer isn't A, B or C

Sometimes he comes back with a totally different solution from outside the options you listed. It's usually the right one.

05Things to Avoid

CAUTION

Anything that doesn't make sense

This is the one thing that won't get through. Talk that's only about profit, stories that don't add up, things handed over without any thought. He stops right there.

Trying to change a decision from the outside

He doesn't much like having his own decisions changed by other people. If you want something changed, start with the reason. If it makes sense, he'll change it himself.

06Troubleshooting

TROUBLESHOOTING

What it looks likeUsual cause
He looks angry He's doing it on purpose. He's just putting it across in a way that reaches the person who needs to hear it, so you can take it at face value.
He seems to be in a bad mood His mood hardly ever swings. In his own words, "You don't need to have moods." Usually nothing is going on that's worth the worry around him.
He suddenly goes silent / is slow to reply This is rare. If it happens, he's sleepy or hungry. See 02 and 07.
You asked him for something, but he isn't moving It may be how you asked (04). Dumping it all on him, no alternative, a task anyone could do. It's often one of those.

07Food

MEALS

Delicious food. But it depends on the quality. Quantity won't recharge him.

What works best is home cooking.

08Days Off

DAY OFF

He goes to get his car washed, has lunch on the way, and comes home. Nothing special needed. A clean car and a good meal, and that's plenty.

As long as it makes sense, he'll almost always move.
