Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT:
how I use the 3 AIs I pay for

2026.10.21 · Ai Murata (Manager)

I subscribe to 3 AIs: Claude, Gemini, and ChatGPT. Gemini and ChatGPT are both paid plans at about 3,000 yen a month.

But honestly, Claude is the only one I use every day right now. Today I’ll write about how I use the 3 (or rather, how I’ve been using 2 of them less).

Gemini can “watch” YouTube

What sets Gemini apart most is that it watches YouTube videos for you. Paste a video’s URL, ask “What’s this video saying?”, and it sums up the content.

As far as I know, Claude and ChatGPT can’t watch the video itself, even if you paste the URL. Gemini is made by Google, and YouTube belongs to Google too, so I guess they’re a natural fit.

There’s one thing that tripped me up. When I pasted a URL copied with the share button on my phone, Gemini told me it couldn’t view it. URLs from the share button have “?si=...” added at the end. I deleted that part and pasted it again, and Gemini watched it just fine.

If it can’t view the video

  • https://youtu.be/xxxxx?si=yyyyy → Delete “?si=” and everything after it, then paste again

It’s handy when you want to know “So what’s the point?” of a long seminar video. But that’s pretty much all I use it for, and I haven’t opened it much lately.

ChatGPT, for images now and then

I use ChatGPT to make images once in a while.

But the pictures just scream “AI made this.” They’re slick and look like something you’ve seen somewhere before. Use them on a flyer or Instagram, and people instantly think, “Oh, that’s AI.” So I’m using it less here too, and I’m thinking about what to do with the subscription.

I hear ChatGPT also released something new called “Dot,” but I haven’t tried it yet.

“Assistant” vs. “agent”

You often hear people split them up like this: Gemini is an AI assistant, and Claude and ChatGPT are AI agents. Honestly, I didn’t really get it either, so I asked Claude to sort it out for me.

  • Assistant = answers what you ask“What does this mean?” “Sum up this video.” → You get an answer. After that, the hands-on work is on you.
  • Agent = does the job you gave it, hands-on, until it’s finished“Make this month’s transfer list.” → It opens the files it needs, copies the numbers, and finishes the table.

To put it another way, an assistant is a know-it-all who can answer anything, and an agent is an employee you can hand work to.

But lately every AI can both “answer” and “do,” so apparently the line is pretty blurry. So rather than flatly saying “Gemini isn’t an agent,” it might be more accurate to say “The way I use them, I just ask Gemini things, and I hand work to Claude.”

Right now, I want to go all in on Claude Code

What I use most is Claude Code, part of Claude. It has “Code” in the name, but it’s not just a programming tool. I have it read files on my computer, fix Excel sheets, and draft invoices and transfer lists. It’s exactly the “employee” type.

New AIs and new features come out practically every month. There’s no end to chasing them all, so I decided to focus on mastering Claude Code for now. Gemini and ChatGPT are at a distance: I open them when I need them.

Summary

  • Gemini Watches YouTube videos and sums them up. That’s pretty much all I use it for
  • ChatGPT Used now and then for images. But it feels very AI, so I’m rethinking how to use it
  • Claude (Claude Code) Takes the work as is. This is my one focus right now

Paying for all 3 might be a bit of a waste. I may review one of the subscriptions around next month. If I do, I’ll write about it here.
