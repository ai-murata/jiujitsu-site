Having Claude review a year of invoices

2026.09.28 Ai Murata (Manager)

Hi, this is Ai Murata. Post number 4 is about how Claude saved me. To be honest, it's also a story about my own mistake.

The monthly invoices are incredibly detailed

We run YAWARA and SWEEP under service contracts, so every month I make invoices for that contract work.

These invoices are incredibly detailed. It's not just the contract fee. They also include line-by-line details of every personal lesson.

  • Who bought which personal lessons
  • When they first used them
  • How much they cost, and what percentage of that applies

I add this up person by person, every month. Since it's money, this is work that absolutely can't have mistakes.

And lately, there's been more and more in there.

What goes into the monthly invoices

  • Contract fee
  • Personal lesson details (YAWARA and SWEEP)
  • Ryozo Top Team plans (both YAWARA and SWEEP)
  • Sometimes, the Instructor Course
  • Things we bought and paid for up front

I've been putting all these invoices together by myself every month.

As long as people do it, mistakes happen

I know I really should set things up so that it isn't all left to one person. But in reality, I've been doing it alone all this time.

So this time, I had Claude look back through about a year of past invoices.

And it found mistakes in some of them. Right now, I'm getting things in place to correct them.

Without Claude,
I'd never have noticed these mistakes in my lifetime.

That thought gave me chills. Because money is involved, I really want to get this right. I felt that more strongly than ever.

Accurate, reliable, fast

What impressed me most this time was its ability to go back through all the past data in one go.

Rechecking a year of invoices by eye would take days. And since I'm human too, I might miss the same spots again. Claude went through it all in no time, and lined up every little detail.

Honestly, I now think Claude is more correct, more reliable, and faster than I am at this.

Even so, my own eyes get the last look

That said, if I leave everything to Claude, I might not notice when something big goes wrong. So right now, I always check everything Claude makes with my own eyes at the end.

  • Totals and cross-checking go to Claude
  • I do the final check on everything that comes out
  • Extra care wherever money moves

Claude makes it fast and accurately, and I take responsibility for checking it. So far, this division of roles is what gives me the most peace of mind.

A truly important partner

As for my own mistake, I feel like, "Well, I really blew it." I'll correct things properly with everyone involved.

With all that said, what today taught me is that Claude is a truly important partner I need to keep learning to use well. The accuracy, and the power to zip back through past data. It was a day of being genuinely impressed.

If you're anxious about carrying your monthly paperwork all by yourself, try starting with having your past work reviewed once. I'm sure it'll catch things you couldn't have noticed yourself.
