If you can’t use it at work, use it at home

2026.09.28 Ai Murata (Manager)

Hi, this is Ai Murata. Post number 5 is for people who aren't using Claude Code.

"Company rules say I can't use it"

Every so often I hear this from people who work at big companies.

I want to use Claude, but I can't,
because our internal rules aren't in place yet.

I understand this feeling so well. I used to work as university staff myself, so I know firsthand what it's like inside an organization.

Security really is a big issue. When you handle company and customer information, one employee can't just start using something because it looks handy. I completely understand being stuck inside that "you can't" limit.

So try it outside of work

Still, I think it's a waste not to touch it at all just because you can't use it at work.

If you can't use it at work, try something outside of work. If you look, I'm sure there's something in your life where you think, "Couldn't this be a bit easier?"

My case: 4 years of paper recipes

For example, what came to me today was my cooking class recipes.

I've been going to a cooking class for about 4 years now. The recipes we get each time are on paper, and before I knew it I had probably hundreds of sheets.

There are still dishes I remember and want to make again, but finding and looking through those papers is such a hassle.

So here's what I'm planning to try.

  • STEP 1Scan the paper recipesEven with hundreds of sheets, it starts with turning them into data.
  • STEP 2Put them all on one siteSearchable by dish name, easy to look up right away.
  • STEP 3Talk to my teacherCould we set it up so she can sell her recipes and earn income from them?

Of course, the recipes belong to my teacher, so first I'll talk it over with her properly.

I want "my teacher's flavors"

There are endless recipe sites now, Cookpad and many others. But what I want to see isn't "some recipe from somewhere."

  • I love my teacher's flavors, so I want to see her recipes
  • People in the class can review, like "what was that dish again?"
  • If the recipes sell, my teacher properly earns income too

I'd love for it to become that kind of place, so I'm starting on it now. When it's done, I'll report back here.

Ideas come when I'm spacing out

What I'm trying to say is: if you can't use it at work, look for ways to make your own life easier.

By the way, I think about these things while spacing out during time away from my phone, like when I'm at the gym. It's when there's no screen in my hands that things like "that's a pain" and "can't something be done about this?" come to mind.

While you can't use it at work,
it's your chance to practice in daily life.

When your company's rules are finally in place, you'll be in a strong spot if it already feels natural in your hands. Start by finding one small hassle around you.
